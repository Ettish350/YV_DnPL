## Decision
Generated metadata reaches main only through a pull request. Lior or Anna merging it is the approval to deploy. Nothing is deployed from a branch.

## DD source / evidence
Standing rule: no deployment without Lior's or Anna's explicit approval.

## Alternatives considered
Approval in chat or email: leaves no link between what was approved and what was deployed.
